## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sat 5 Sep · Landing day
> They land tired, and by the time they clear Heathrow it's already early afternoon. A near-the-hotel lunch, a slow drift into pub drinks, and a proper Marylebone dinner to end the night.

- ✈️ **Land LHR 11:30am** on United UA5 from Houston.
- 🚕 **Immigration, bags, and Uber into town.** Figure ~1 hour clearing the airport, then a 45-60 min Uber. Realistic central London arrival: **~1:30-2:00pm**.
- 🛏️ **Check into the Twenty Two Hotel.** Drop bags, freshen up. (Confirm the hotel's check-in time. If it's a standard 3pm and they arrive earlier, plan for a slow coffee nearby rather than a hard wait in the lobby.)
- 🍴 **Late lunch at Dover Street Market Counter**, near the hotel. (Reminder set to book this 12 Aug.)
- 🍺 **~6:00pm: pints at the Hart, Marylebone.** Low-key, no rush, exactly the register a jet-lagged first evening needs.
- 🍴 **~8:30pm: dinner at Nina, Marylebone.** Closes the night properly instead of drifting back to the lunch spot.

> ⭐ **The Move:** Hart → Nina. It turns "figure out dinner while exhausted" into one clean plan: pints first, a table already lined up after.
> 💡 **Note:** martini night (Duke's/Connaught) is intentionally NOT today, saving that one for when they're more rested.

### Day 2 · Sun 6 Sep · Notting Hill day
> A market morning, a burger everyone already wants, and a proper roast to close the day.

- 🍴 **12:00pm: Lunch at the Dove.** Booked. Keep it light, this is a market-day lunch, not a destination meal.
- 🛍️ **Afternoon: Portobello Road Market.** Browse, no fixed agenda.

### Day 3 · Mon 7 Sep · Borough Market, Tate Modern, Kutir
> The biggest day of the trip, and now a real plan instead of an open question: market, museum, Michelin dinner.

- 🛍️ **Late morning: Borough Market.** Go before the lunch crowd builds.
- 🍴 **~12:30pm: Lunch at Oma.** Booked via calendar reminder (7 Aug). Right at the market, no travel between the two.
- 👀 **Early-mid afternoon: Tate Modern.** A five-minute walk over the Millennium Bridge from the market, so it costs nothing extra to get there.
- 🍴 **~7:30-8:00pm: Dinner at Kutir, Chelsea.** Michelin-starred, tasting-menu format, calmer room than Gymkhana for a group of 3-4 at the end of a full day. (Gymkhana is the backup if the group wants buzzier over calmer.)

> ⭐ **The Move:** Kutir. One Michelin-caliber Indian dinner, chosen deliberately over the louder, harder-to-book Gymkhana.
> 🎯 **Book it:** Kutir, table for 3-4, evening of the 7th.
